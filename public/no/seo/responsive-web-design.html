Deep Dive

Why Responsive Design Matters for Your Google Rankings

Google uses mobile-first indexing — meaning it crawls and ranks your website based on how it performs on mobile, not desktop. A site that looks great on desktop but breaks on mobile will rank lower regardless of content quality. devndespro builds mobile-first from the ground up.

What True Responsive Design Looks Like

  • Fluid layouts that adapt to any screen width — not just fixed breakpoints
  • Images served in the right size and format for each device
  • Touch-friendly interactions and tap targets on mobile
  • Font sizes and spacing that read clearly at all viewport sizes
  • Navigation patterns that work on both touch and mouse/keyboard
