What Small Businesses Need from a Website

A small business website has one primary job: convert visitors into customers or enquiries. Everything else — design aesthetics, animations, clever copy — is secondary to this function. We design and build small business websites with this conversion goal at the centre of every decision.

Small Business Website Features We Include

  • Mobile-first design that works on all smartphones
  • Local SEO setup — Google Business Profile, local schema, city-specific keywords
  • Kontakt forms connected to your email inbox
  • WhatsApp chat button for instant customer contact
  • Google Maps integration for location-based businesses
  • Google Analytics and Search Console setup and configuration
  • Page speed optimisation for fast load times on mobile networks
