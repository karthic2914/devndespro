Nettsidedesign for Norway

Norwegian businesses need websites that perform technically and convert commercially. We build React and Next.js websites optimised for Google.no rankings, Norwegian mobile audiences, and the dual challenge of serving both local Norwegian and international customers effectively.

What Norwegian Businesses Need from Nettsidedesign

Norwegian businesses operate in a market with among the highest digital expectations in the world. Norwegian consumers are early adopters of technology, expect fast and seamless digital experiences, and make credibility judgements based on website quality before they ever contact a business.

Norwegian Nettsidedesign Standards

Web design for Norway means meeting specific technical and regulatory standards. GDPR compliance is mandatory. Accessibility standards WCAG 2.1 AA is a legal requirement for many Norwegian businesses. Vipps integration is expected for consumer-facing Norwegian e-commerce. And performance on Norwegian mobile networks must be considered at the design stage.

Local SEO for Norwegian Cities

Ranking on Google.no for city-specific queries requires proper LocalBusiness schema, Google Business Profile optimisation, Norwegian-language keyword research, and consistent NAP information across Norwegian directories. These are all part of our standard delivery for Norwegian clients.
