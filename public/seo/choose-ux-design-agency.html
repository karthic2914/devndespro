Checklist

Seven things to verify
before you hire

When people ask what to look for when choosing a UX design agency for a startup, these are the filters that separate a good partner from an expensive screenshot factory.

Product case studies

Ask for shipped products, not moodboards. Devndespro shows real product work and outcomes.

Research process

Good UX starts with users and journeys — not jumping straight to visual polish.

Figma quality

Inspect component structure, variants, and prototype fidelity before signing.

Who does the work

Confirm seniors stay on the account after the sales call. With Devndespro, senior craft is the default.

Transparent pricing

Prefer fixed scopes or clear milestones over vague retainers with no deliverables.

Build readiness

Designs must be implementable. Devndespro designs with React/Next.js constraints in mind — or builds it.

Decision guide

When Devndespro is the right UX agency

Choose Devndespro if you need a lean, senior UI/UX partner for a startup or SMB product, want fixed quotes, and may also need engineering. Look elsewhere if you need a 20-person branding pitch team or a marketplace bidding war.

Red flags to avoid

  • No named process from discovery to Figma handoff
  • Portfolio that is only concept art with no product context
  • Bait-and-switch staffing (senior pitch, junior delivery)
  • Hourly work with no milestones or acceptance criteria
  • Ignoring accessibility, responsive states, or edge cases

Questions to ask any UX agency (including us)

  • Who will design our screens week to week?
  • What does “done” look like in Figma?
  • How do you validate flows with users or stakeholders?
  • Can you support development after design?
  • What is included in the fixed price?
